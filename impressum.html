About the app

“Wo ist Roland?” is a private, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with Rügensche BäderBahn (RüBB), Eisenbahn-Bau- und Betriebsgesellschaft Pressnitztalbahn mbH or the district of Vorpommern-Rügen. Train positions are calculated from the published timetable, not from GPS on the trains. Deviations, delays and cancellations are possible; the information given by RüBB on site and at ruegensche-baederbahn.de prevails.
